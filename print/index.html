Allergies and dietary needs

Please tell your server about any dietary restrictions or food allergies. We strongly advise guests with severe food allergies or sensitivities to exercise caution when dining at Tabu Shabu. We make every effort to accommodate food allergies and keep our guests safe, but cross-contamination is always possible and we cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment. If you have any questions, please ask your server.
